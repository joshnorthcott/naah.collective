A two-bedroom lock-off apartment in Ameka Urban Condos, a boutique building of just 50 residences in Montealbán, north Mérida — 89 m² of interior space, two en-suite bedrooms and two parking spaces, with delivery in June 2027.

The plan is what sets it apart. The two bedrooms sit at opposite ends of the apartment, each with its own bathroom, and the second suite comes with its own TV room. Designed as a lock-off, that side can be closed off from the rest — live in one part and rent the other, host family with real privacy, or let the two independently.

Between them, an open living and dining room runs into the kitchen, with a guest half bath and a laundry area off to the side. The finishes are clean and warm: large-format 60 × 120 cm porcelain floors, black window frames, a dark granite kitchen worktop, dark marble in the bathrooms, wood doors and bathroom furniture, Urrea fittings and tempered-glass shower screens, with designer lighting throughout.

This is a resale of a pre-construction unit, offered below what the developer is now asking for the same model — a way into the building today at an earlier price. Images on this page are the developer’s renders of the model.

Montealbán

Ameka Urban Condos rises seven levels in Montealbán, one of north Mérida’s most established and best-valued neighbourhoods, inside the Periférico and close to the Glorieta del Pocito. Plaza Solare, Plaza Patio and the restaurants along Avenida Cámara de Comercio are a few minutes away, Prolongación Montejo is close by, and the hospitals and shops of Altabrisa are a short drive east. The building shares a pool with a sun deck and grill, a rooftop terrace and sport bar, a coworking room, a pet spa and pet park, bicycle parking and delivery lockers, with QR access, CCTV, lifts and an emergency generator.
