Villa 36

A stunning single-storey residence of 364.99 m², set on a generous 725.75 m² on a quiet private street inside the prestigious Yucatán Country Club.

$16,700,000 MXN approx. USD $982,000

Built in 2021 and elegantly resolved on a single level, so the entire house opens onto the same lush private garden.

The plan is beautifully minimalist and generously proportioned: living, dining and kitchen flow together as one long, light-filled space, framed by floor-to-ceiling reflective glass along the garden side and opening onto an inviting covered terrace. Warm beige 60 × 60 porcelain runs seamlessly through every room, walls and ceilings are finished in smooth three-coat plaster, and the joinery — doors, closets, the fully fitted kitchen — is rich solid hardwood throughout.

From the street the house is discreet and striking in equal measure: slatted timber walls beneath a dramatic cantilevered roof that sweeps on over the parking and the entry path. Behind it the plan turns inward to embrace an expansive hardwood deck and a sculptural stone fountain, framed by mature palms, banana and fern. Skylights pour daylight into the kitchen and the spa-like main bathroom, and every room looks out onto green.

The deck was also built pool-ready: the ground beneath it is already excavated, so an owner who wants a pool need only lift the boards and build — no digging, no disruption. A whole-house standby generator is installed, and the property is turnkey and immaculate, ready to occupy as it stands.

Yucatán Country Club

The house sits within the prestigious Yucatán Country Club, an exceptional 325-hectare community at kilometre 15.5 of the Mérida–Progreso highway, laid out around the celebrated El Jaguar — a magnificent eighteen-hole Jack Nicklaus Signature course threaded through four lakes, five natural cenotes and Maya archaeological remains, and host to the Mundo Maya Open on the PGA Tour Latinoamérica.

More than five hundred families call it home. The elegant Casa Club offers a full spa and gymnasium, adult and children's pools, fine restaurants and cafés, a bar and wine cellar, a children's play centre, and six sports academies covering golf, tennis and paddle. Access is gated and patrolled around the clock, the beaches at Progreso are roughly fifteen minutes north, and Mérida's thriving northern corridor is a short run south.
