A three-bedroom family house in Cholul, set behind an electric gate with a massive garden, a covered terrace and a pool finished in chukum — freshly painted and waterproofed from top to bottom, and ready to move into.

The ground floor is planned around the garden. A long hall runs from the front door straight through to the glass at the back, and the living room opens onto the lawn and the pool through full-height sliding doors. The dining room is a room of its own rather than a corner of the lounge, with space for a proper table.

The kitchen is spacious, with granite counters and backsplash, a long run of upper and lower cabinets in a warm wood tone, and a window over the sink. Each of the three bedrooms has full fitted closets (the principal a walk-in dressing room) and its own full bathroom, with a glass-screened shower and a stone vanity top. Downstairs there is a half bathroom for visitors.

It has just been looked after properly: the whole house repainted and the roof waterproofed, every air-conditioning unit serviced, and new blinds hung throughout. There is a linen closet, a separate laundry room, and a covered garage for two cars behind the electric gate.

Garden, terrace & pool

The garden is the reason to live here — a massive lawn running back to a line of mature trees, with a roofed terrace off the living room for meals outside through the rainy season. The pool sits right beside the terrace and is finished in chukum, the traditional Yucatecan lime-and-resin render that stays cool underfoot and turns the water a soft turquoise.

Cholul

Cholul sits just outside the Periférico on Mérida's north-east side — an old village whose square and church are still at its heart, now ringed by quiet residential streets. It keeps a calmer pace than the city while staying close to it: Altabrisa, Gran Plaza and the hospitals and schools of the north are a short drive back across the ring road, and the Progreso highway is within easy reach for weekends on the coast.
