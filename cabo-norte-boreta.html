A newly built contemporary house in Boreta, one of the private enclaves of Cabo Norte — 635 m² over three levels, six bedrooms and six bathrooms, with a hand-laid stone façade set among mature native trees.

From the street, the house reads as a white volume resting on a base of warm stone. A short flight of stone steps rises to a solid-wood front door, while the drive drops beneath it to a basement garage for three cars, which also holds a small gym. Above, the rooms climb through three levels with wide black-framed windows looking into the trees on every side, and the roof is finished as a rooftop with room for an outdoor living and entertaining area.

The main living level is open and generous: a living room beneath a warm timber ceiling, a kitchen with a long dark-stone island and breakfast bar, and a separate dining room looking out into the garden. Floors are polished cement throughout, and the joinery is solid wood — floor-to-ceiling shelving, doors and walk-in wardrobes. Upstairs, the bedrooms are expansive and light-filled, and the bathrooms pair stone-clad walls with floating wood vanities and walk-in showers behind black-framed glass.

The house also has an interior garden, a laundry room and service quarters. It is sold fully furnished and in its current state: construction is complete, with a few finishing details still to resolve — which lets a buyer choose those finishes to their own taste.

The land

The lot is 265 m², planted with native trees and palms that wrap the house in green. Building both down — a basement for parking — and up to a rooftop gives the house 635 m² of construction on a compact footprint, with a monthly maintenance fee of $2,567 MXN for the privada.

Cabo Norte

Cabo Norte is one of north Mérida’s most sought-after master-planned communities: more than 160 hectares just north of the Periférico, laid out around living lakes, parks and green walking trails, with gated private enclaves and 24-hour security. Its amenities include a clubhouse with a lap pool and a lakeside deck, padel and tennis courts and a games room. The La Isla Mérida shopping centre sits within the development, and schools, hospitals and restaurants are a short drive away.
