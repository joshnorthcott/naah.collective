A brand-new two-bedroom apartment on the second level, north-facing over the gardens and the pool, with its own private rooftop reached by a spiral stair — 112 m² in the heart of Temozón Norte.

Nothing here has been lived in yet. Travertine-toned porcelain runs the full length of the apartment, from the open living and dining room through to the bedrooms, and the north orientation keeps it cool and evenly lit — the windows look into the crowns of the trees rather than at a neighbouring wall.

The kitchen is generous for an apartment of this size, with a granite bar that doubles as the dining counter, chocolate-toned carpentry and a full run of upper cabinets. A separate laundry room off the hall takes the water heater and the washer, so nothing is tucked into a cupboard.

Both bedrooms are air-conditioned, fitted with blackout blinds and carry their own closets — the principal a walk-in dressing room, the second a fitted wardrobe with drawers. Each has a full bathroom finished in travertine, with a frameless glass shower and a stone vanity top.

Tuscania Temozón Norte

Temozón Norte is the address most people mean when they say north Mérida — the stretch above the Periférico on the Progreso road, where the city's newest restaurants, schools and shopping sit within a few minutes of each other. Altabrisa, Up Town, the UADY campus and City Center are all a short drive, and Progreso and the coast are half an hour up the highway.

Tuscania itself is built around a hotel-style pool set in mature gardens, with a shaded pavilion, lounge terrace, lawn and a children's play area, and security on the gate around the clock. The apartment looks straight out over it.

The pool, gardens and play area are shared by the residents of Tuscania.
