A furnished one-bedroom apartment of 51 m² on the second level of Triventto, in Sodzil Norte — turnkey, with a rooftop pool and bar upstairs and Plaza Galerías, Costco and The Harbor a few minutes away.

208 is one of a small number of units in the building offered furnished, which is what makes it straightforward: the apartment is finished, equipped and ready to move into or to put to work, with no fit-out to organise from abroad and nothing left to buy.

The layout is a single open run — living room, dining area and a fully equipped kitchen along one wall — opening onto the balcony, with the bedroom set behind it. The bedroom carries a walk-in closet, and the bathroom is finished in stone and dark porcelain, with a stone vanity top and a round backlit mirror.

At 51 m² the apartment is compact by design, and what it buys is the rest of the building: a lounge pool and roof top bar with the whole north of the city in front of them, a gym, a co-working room, a coffee bar off the lobby and a concierge at the door.

Triventto

Triventto is a completed building of 37 one-bedroom apartments and six lock-off penthouse duplexes, developed by BREHCO on a quiet street in Sodzil Norte. It is finished and occupied — the pool, the bar, the gym and the lobby are all built and in use, which is a different proposition from buying off plan.

Hacienda Sodzil Norte sits in the north of the city, inside the Periférico and a couple of streets off Prolongación Montejo — close enough to the ring road to leave town quickly, far enough in to be quiet. Plaza Galerías, Costco, The Harbor and Universidad Marista are all within a few minutes' drive, City Center and Plaza San Ángelo are close behind, and the airport is about twenty-five minutes across town. It is the part of Mérida that has appreciated fastest, and the reason a one-bedroom here rents as easily as it sells.

Parking sits under the building, off a quiet residential street.
