Casa Santa Lucía · Nortemérida

Komchén, north of Mérida, Yucatán

For sale · 600 m² lot

A 490 m² family house on a 600 m² lot, built around a nine-metre double-height living room — four en-suite bedrooms, a pool on several levels, and its own solar power with battery backup.

The ground floor opens as one tall volume: living and dining rise the full nine metres against a marble feature wall, with a floating walkway crossing overhead. The kitchen is built around a granite island that seats five, with an induction hob and an extractor hood rising from the island itself, and a full set of SMEG appliances — oven, microwave, coffee machine and dishwasher. A pantry, a tzalam-wood wine rack, a guest powder room, a laundry room and a service room with its own bath complete the floor.

One bedroom sits on the ground floor, with its walk-in closet and bathroom — useful for guests or for anyone who would rather avoid the stairs. Upstairs, two further bedrooms each have a walk-in closet and bathroom, one opening onto a terrace over the garden and pool, and a TV room sits off the walkway.

The principal suite takes the rest of the upper floor: a large window over the park, its own terrace, a dressing room with a central island, and a bathroom with a double vanity, enclosed WC, shower and a separate bath room with a massage tub. Every bathroom counter in the house is clad in marble.

Residencial Nortemérida

Nortemérida is a gated residential development north of the city, outside the Periférico beside Komchén and just west of the Mérida–Progreso highway. The house sits in Privada Santa Lucía, one of its closed sections, and looks out over a park. The highway puts the north-side shopping and hospitals within easy reach going south, and the beach at Progreso going north.
