Beachfront residence · Telchac Puerto, Yucatán

A light-filled beachfront residence at Kinuh, where the turquoise Gulf fills the glass and a private plunge pool sits on its own 45 m² terrace.

The apartment occupies 212 m² on the first level, facing north to open sea. The principal bedroom and the living room look straight out to the water; the two secondary bedrooms turn toward the lagoon and the palms behind. Sliding glass runs the length of the social area, so the terrace reads as another room rather than a balcony.

The plan is unusually generous for a beach apartment: three bedrooms, each with a king bed, a separate service room with its own full bathroom, two television rooms, and four and a half bathrooms in all. A second fitted kitchen lets part of the apartment close off as a lock-off — useful for guests, or for keeping a rental running while the family is in residence.

It is sold furnished, and the furniture was made for it: bed bases, headboards, nightstands and tables in parota, cedar and tzalam, and a dining table cut from a single red cedar root. Travertine runs across the floors and up the shower walls. Air conditioning and ceiling fans throughout, blinds on every window, walk-in closets in the bedrooms, and two covered parking spaces.

The development

Kinuh sits on the sand at Telchac Puerto, a little over an hour from Mérida and twenty minutes beyond Progreso, on the quieter stretch of the Yucatán coast. The building steps back from the dune in white terraces, with a lap pool and a second family pool between it and the beach, a palapa bar, sun decks, a children's play area, and a roped sand path running from the pool deck straight down to the water. Covered parking, elevator and controlled access.
