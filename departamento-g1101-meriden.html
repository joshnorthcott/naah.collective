Meriden, Cabo Norte, Mérida, Yucatán

Per month · maintenance included · fully furnished

A two-bedroom apartment at Meriden, inside the Cabo Norte master community north of Mérida — 120.6 m² indoors opening onto a 30.3 m² terrace above Lake Liverpool, with a second balcony on the far side of the apartment facing the clubhouse lake and the green belt behind it.

The terrace is the room the apartment is arranged around. It runs the full width of the living area behind floor-to-ceiling glass, with space for a dining table and a lounge group at the same time, and the principal bedroom opens directly onto it. The water below is Lake Liverpool; beyond it the city spreads out low and green.

Inside, the living and dining areas share one open space with the fitted kitchen, which comes with its own hob, oven and extractor hood. The principal bedroom takes the terrace view and has a walk-in dressing room and a full bathroom. A hallway lined with a deep linen closet leads to the second bedroom, which has its own balcony, its own dressing room, and a full bathroom reachable from both the bedroom and the hall.

The fit-out is a step above the usual rental. Every room and living area is air conditioned, the windows are hurricane-rated and tinted, every door and drawer closes on a soft-close mechanism, and the bathrooms are fitted with Helvex. Behind the kitchen there is a service area with its own washroom and laundry sink, and the compressor room beside it is large enough to be used as internal storage. A double parking space directly in front of the tower lifts comes with the apartment. An intercom connects straight through to the condominium gatehouse, and only two apartments share each floor — the neighbouring door faces this one across the landing.

Cabo Norte

Meriden sits inside Cabo Norte, the master-planned community just north of Mérida and immediately outside the Periférico, off the Prolongación Montejo axis. Access is by facial recognition throughout, there are two security gatehouses — one for Cabo Norte and one for Meriden itself — and the lobby is staffed from 8am to 8pm. Residents also have the amenities of the wider Cabo Norte community, whose lakes, walking circuits and clubhouse are shared across the development.
