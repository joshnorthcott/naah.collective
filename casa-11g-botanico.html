A light-filled three-bedroom house behind a stone facade, with a 3 × 9 m lap pool fed by its own well and a covered terrace that opens off the living room — ten minutes from everything on Mérida's north side.

The plan is generous and uncomplicated: a single run of living and dining that ends in a wall of glass onto the garden, a separate equipped kitchen with a granite bar, a laundry area and a guest powder room. Sixty-centimetre porcelain floors run throughout, and the semi-luxury finish level shows in the details — cedar-fronted carpentry, tempered-glass shower enclosures, ivory cream marble vanity tops.

Upstairs there are three bedrooms, each with its own bathroom. The principal has a double vanity and a full walk-in dressing room; a family room at the top of the stairs takes the afternoon light and works equally well as a study. Every room is screened, air-conditioned and fitted with curtains and a ceiling fan.

The house is fully serviced for life here — its own well, a water softener and reverse-osmosis system, a pressuriser, gas boiler and stationary tank, and automated irrigation for the garden. It is held free of liens and can be bought outright or with Mexican bank financing.

Residencial Botánico

Botánico is a gated development in Conkal, on the north edge of Mérida — the side of the city that has appreciated fastest and is best served. Supermarkets, banks, restaurants and schools are five to ten minutes away, along with the UADY campus, Plaza Altabrisa, Up Town and Star Médica.

The monthly fee covers nine common parks, a football pitch, two padel courts, a gym, a games pavilion and a children's playground, plus 24/7 security, exterior fumigation, landscape maintenance, the automated gate and administration.
